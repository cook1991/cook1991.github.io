Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

AuthVault does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, usage data, or analytics — from you or about you. There is nothing to disclose because there is nothing collected.

No internet access

The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission. It has no network code and cannot send data anywhere even if it tried.

What's stored, and where

Your two-factor account names and secret keys are encrypted and stored only in the app's local, private storage on your device, using a key held in the Android Keystore. Nothing leaves the device automatically.

Backups

If you choose to export a backup, that file is written only to a location you pick on your own device (or a cloud-storage folder you've mounted there) and is encrypted with a passphrase you set. We never receive a copy.

Camera

The app requests camera access solely to scan 2FA QR codes you point it at. No image or video is stored or transmitted; it's decoded on-device and discarded immediately.
