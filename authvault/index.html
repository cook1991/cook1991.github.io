Why another authenticator app?

Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, and Authy all ask for permissions and cloud sync a local code generator has no real need for. AuthVault doesn't request the internet permission at all — your two-factor secrets can't leave the device even if something tried to make them.

🔢

TOTP & HOTP

Time-based and counter-based one-time codes, for sites that still issue HOTP hardware-token-style secrets.

🚫

No internet permission

The app can't phone home — there's nothing in the manifest that lets it try.

🔒

Encrypted local vault

Secrets are encrypted at rest using Android Keystore-backed keys, unlocked by your device's own lock screen.

💾

Encrypted backup & restore

Move to a new phone with a passphrase-protected backup file — no account, no cloud.

📷

Import from Google Authenticator

Reads the standard "Transfer accounts" export QR, so switching over doesn't mean re-enrolling every site.

🆓

Free, no ads

It's a utility, not a product with a business model.
