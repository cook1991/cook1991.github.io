How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android above.
  2. Open the downloaded MobileBrowser-1.5.apk file (check your notifications or Downloads).
  3. If Android warns about installing unknown apps, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install. If Google Play Protect says it doesn't recognize the developer, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  4. Open Mobile Browser. To use it for every link, go to Settings and choose Set as default browser.

What you get

  • Phishing warnings. Shows a red warning before opening a known scam site. The list updates daily.
  • Tracker blocking. Blocks known tracker and ad hosts using a list that updates by itself.
  • Private tabs. Private tabs keep their cookies apart from your regular tabs and are wiped when you close the last one.
  • HTTPS first. Moves sites to a secure connection when it can, and warns you when it can't.
  • Your choice per site. Sites have to ask before using your camera, microphone or location.
  • The everyday things. Tabs, bookmarks, history, downloads, find in page, desktop site and a Senior display mode.

Your data

History and bookmarks stay on your phone. The app downloads its block lists from the internet, and it has no analytics or account.

Android only. This app can't be installed on an iPhone. No browser catches every scam, so stay careful with links you weren't expecting.
