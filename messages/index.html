How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android above.
  2. Open the downloaded SpritzLive-Messages-1.12.apk file (check your notifications or Downloads).
  3. If Android warns about installing unknown apps, tap Settings and turn on Allow from this source, then go back and tap Install.
  4. Open Messages, allow the permissions it asks for, and tap Set default to make it your texting app.

What you get

  • Real texting. Send and receive normal SMS text messages as your phone's default messaging app.
  • Photos & video (MMS). Attach several pictures or a short video, and see what people send you.
  • Easy to get around. A big, clear Back to Messages button in every conversation — designed to be easy for everyone.
  • Contact names. Shows who's texting you, newest conversations first.

Android only. This app can't be installed on an iPhone. Setting it as your default SMS app means it takes over texting from your current messaging app — you can switch back anytime in Settings → Apps → Default apps → SMS app.
