Everything, in one quiet app

No sign-up, no tracking, no ads. Just the Word — to read or to hear.

The complete Bible

All 73 books of the Douay-Rheims Catholic Bible, in order, Genesis to Apocalypse — including the deuterocanonical books and the classic footnotes.

Search any verse

Instantly search all 35,804 verses for a word or phrase; tap a result to jump straight to it, with your search terms highlighted.

Works fully offline

Every verse is bundled in the app. No account, no internet, no data use — ready wherever you are.

Listen hands-free

Built-in narration reads aloud through your speaker or Bluetooth, with the screen off, rolling chapter-to-chapter like an audiobook.

Follows along

As it reads, the current verse highlights and the page scrolls itself — and you can tap any verse to start there.

Sleep timer

Drift off to Scripture. Playback pauses on its own after a set time, or at the end of the chapter.

Made for reading

Adjustable text size, night mode, and reading-speed control — and it always remembers exactly where you left off.

See it

A calm reader by day, an audiobook by night.

Your privacy: nothing collected

No accounts, no analytics, no trackers, no ads. The entire Bible lives on your device, so the app runs with no internet connection at all — nothing you read or listen to ever leaves your phone. The only time it opens the internet is if you tap “Buy me a coffee,” which simply launches your browser.

Free, and meant to stay that way

Scripture text and footnotes are courtesy of drbo.org. The app is offered free with no ads and nothing locked away. If it blesses you, a coffee ☕ is always appreciated.
