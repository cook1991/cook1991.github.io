Douay-Rheims BiblePrivacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective 19 July 2026

The short version: the Douay-Rheims Bible app does not collect, store, or share any personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no trackers, and no ads. It works entirely on your device.

This policy explains how the Douay-Rheims Bible Android app (“the app”) handles information. It applies to the app only.

Information the app collects

None. The app has no account system, no analytics or crash reporting, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. It does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, device identifiers, or any record of what you read or listen to.

How the app uses the internet

The complete Bible text is bundled inside the app and stored on your device, so the app works fully offline and makes no network requests on its own. The only time it reaches the internet is when you choose to tap the “Buy me a coffee” link, which opens that page in your device’s web browser. From that point you are subject to the privacy practices of your browser and of Buy Me a Coffee; the app does not send them any information about you.

Information stored on your device

Your preferences — last reading position, text size, night-mode setting, and playback speed — are saved only in the app’s private storage on your device so it can remember them between sessions. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Notifications (Android 13+): used only to show playback controls (play/pause, skip, sleep timer) while audio narration is active.
  • Foreground service & wake lock: used only to keep narration playing when the screen is off.

None of these permissions are used to collect or transmit data.

Text-to-speech

Audio narration is produced by the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device (for example, Google Text-to-speech). The app passes verse text to that engine so it can be read aloud. Any processing done by your device’s text-to-speech engine is governed by that engine’s own provider, not by this app.

Children’s privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children, and is suitable for all ages.

Third-party services

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The only external link is the optional Buy Me a Coffee support page described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a revised effective date.
